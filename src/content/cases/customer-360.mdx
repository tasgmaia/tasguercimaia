<Section label="Overview" title="An Omnichannel Customer Relationship Manager for retailers">

As the e-commerce business becomes more competitive, it gets harder for retailers to increase customer lifetime value (CLTV). One of the keys that enable retailers to push themselves above this issue is to deeply identify, understand, and address their customers' needs better and faster than their competitors by knowing the touch points the shopper had with the brand.

In order to address those needs, most e-commerce platforms on the market offer an unstructured view of customer data. This project aimed to create a new information architecture with a data set that was available inside the VTEX platform. By restructuring these data we enabled users to access actionable data from users providing practical information and insights about shoppers.

</Section>

<Section>

The research process had two steps. First, we shared a formulary to understand how customers related to the platform. This research began in a context where the company didn't have a continuous listening program. So we needed to map the perceptions that users had to map experience problems. With this step, we found the need to improve the CX experience. Users had the perception that they could improve their operations by creating a more efficient experience.

Secondly, we promoted a deep dive into CX operations and visited the call centers of customers such as C&A, Drogaria São Paulo, RIHappy, and Exito. These customers represented a diverse base since they had different operations and were focused on different retail markets. From fashion to grocery we were able to see their need onsite.

</Section>

<Figure image={clients} alt="Logos of the visited customers: C&A, Drogaria São Paulo, RIHappy and Exito" narrow />

<Section id="strategy" label="Product strategic modeling" />

<Quote source="Drogaria São Paulo user quote">“CX operators lose time with using multiple tools while lead time is critical to their daily job”</Quote>

<Section>

After these rounds of research, we started a strategic modeling process. We interviewed important stakeholders to understand their points of view regarding the creation of a new product. With this investigation, we delimited the new product to be the single source of truth about shoppers' data.

Then we began a process to map the different sources of data currently available in the platform. With these data, we started the design process to deliver a concise, performative, and smooth experience.

</Section>
